# Audio credits

All audio in In Pieces is recorded or composed by real people and released under CC0 (public domain). No synthesized or AI-generated audio.

- **Sound effects:** Kenney, "Impact Sounds", "RPG Audio" and "Interface Sounds" (https://kenney.nl), CC0.
- **Exploration music:** "Into the Ruined Temple" (OpenGameArt, CC0).
- **Boss music:** "Evil temple Ambiance loop" (OpenGameArt, CC0).

`build_sfx.py` shows exactly which recordings are used and how they were trimmed and encoded.
